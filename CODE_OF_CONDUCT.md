# Code of Conduct

OpenNest is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Assume good intent; give constructive feedback.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). Report concerns to the maintainers. Participants who break these rules may be removed.
